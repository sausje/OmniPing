# On your phone

Open OmniPing's address on your phone, the same one you use on your PC. On a narrow screen it shows a card per target with a sparkline, and any active alerts at the top.

To open it like an app, add it to your home screen: in Safari, **Share → Add to Home Screen**; in Chrome, **⋮ → Add to Home screen**.

- **iPhone:** it opens as an app of its own, with the OmniPing icon.
- **Android:** over plain `http://` on your own network, Chrome opens it in a normal browser tab and puts a small Chrome badge on the icon. Only HTTPS gives an installed app.

For notifications on your phone, see [Alerts and notifications](alerts.md#ntfy-on-your-phone-and-pc).
